About Idrees Travels

A local travel agency in Bara, District Khyber, helping families travel for work, visits and Umrah.

Who We Are

Connecting you to the world

Idrees Travels is a travel agency based at Gul Plaza, Office No. 1, Bara Bazar, District Khyber. We help people from Bara and the surrounding areas book air tickets and plan their Umrah with confidence.

We know that travelling, especially for Umrah, is a big step for every family. That is why we keep things simple: we listen to what you need, explain every option clearly, and stay in touch until you are home again.

  • Domestic & international air tickets
  • Complete Umrah packages for individuals, families & groups
  • Friendly, face-to-face service at our Bara office
